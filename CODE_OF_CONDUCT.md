# Code of Conduct

ANBE is an open-source engineering project.

Participation should be professional, constructive and focused on making
the project better.

## Expected behavior

- discuss technical disagreements respectfully
- criticize ideas and code, not people
- provide useful reproduction details when reporting problems
- respect privacy and do not expose another person's secrets or credentials
- give contributors reasonable space to make mistakes and improve work
- avoid harassment, discrimination and intentionally disruptive behavior

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that are abusive,
malicious, irrelevant or unsafe for the community.

Repeated serious violations may result in participation restrictions.

The goal is simple: keep ANBE a place where technical work can be done
openly and productively.
